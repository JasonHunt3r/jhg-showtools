**Why half speed:** `transitionDrag` (StorylineView.swift:575) uses
`DragGesture(minimumDistance: 2)` in its own, *local* space, but the section
it's attached to moves and widens as it's dragged, so the pointer's
translation is measured against a moving view. The trim edges measure in
`.named("storyline")` for exactly this reason (their comment says so).
Fix: the same named space. ImagesRow.swift:186 has the same pattern.

## Summary

About 60 checks run; everything passes except:

1. **Half-speed drags in the lane (both rows).** Transition sections (edges and
   middle) and lane images (move and trim) follow the pointer at half speed:
   their drag gestures measure in the moving view's own space. One-line fix
   each (`coordinateSpace: .named("storyline")`), as the trim edges already do.
2. **Delete on a selected transition needs the keyboard in Edit Show.** With
   it in the sidebar nothing happens, and clicking a section doesn't move it.
3. **Esc after selecting a lane image in its row** doesn't deselect (Esc is
   only handled by the picture's overlay, which doesn't have the keyboard).
4. **Much of the inspector is below the fold** at the default window size
   (Rotation's slider, Angles/Speed, the pivot Lock); not a bug, but Speed
   mode and the pivot lock went unchecked because of it.

Still for Jason: drags from Finder and Photos, Touch ID (private off,
Unlock), pinch, a second screen, cursors, and how everything looks and feels.
